# Paraguayan Guaraní starter

Choose **Paraguayan Guaraní** in Settings. The beginner list covers nine words about the natural world. The expanded list adds five more. My words and Review use the included vocabulary without a Gemini key. Weaving words into web pages uses the existing Gemini and site access settings.

Spelling and the underlying senses come from the Dirección General de Investigación Lingüística at Paraguay's Secretaría de Políticas Lingüísticas. Its *Nombres propios en guaraní* explains the ordinary words behind names recorded in Paraguay and documents their modern spellings. The starter pairs those words with short English glosses written for Lang Weave, with one sense selected per word. The app displays the target form and its English gloss. [Nombres propios en guaraní, Spanish edition](https://spl.gov.py/files/DGIL/Nombre_propio_%20guarani_ESP.pdf)

Generated Guaraní packs remain available under their existing selections and keep their own progress. The starter has a separate catalog ID and its backup stores progress by that ID. Restore a backup containing the starter selection or progress with Lang Weave 0.12.26 or later.
